# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own the quality assurance strategy, test execution, and acceptance validation. They collaborate with Developers and Product Managers to ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and quality standards for each project
- Create and maintain acceptance test cases and automation frameworks
- Lead QA activities during execution and pre-release phases
- Identify quality risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Partner with Developers on testability and design for quality
- Report quality metrics and blockers to PM and the project team

### Goals
- Ensure features are production-ready and meet acceptance criteria
- Reduce defects in production through thorough testing
- Enable fast, confident releases through test automation

### Typical Communication
- Quality planning during project planning phase
- Weekly quality status in execution syncs
- Test results and blockers in sprint reviews
- Escalation of quality risks or critical defects

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical guidance, review architecture and design decisions, and identify technical risks. They ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with platform standards.

### Responsibilities
- Review and approve technical designs and architecture decisions
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Provide guidance on code quality, performance, and scalability
- Lead design reviews and architecture discussions
- Mentor Developers and support technical problem-solving
- Contribute to Definition of Done and technical standards

### Goals
- Ensure solutions are technically sound and maintainable
- Reduce technical debt and production incidents
- Maintain consistent architecture across projects

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews during planning
- Architecture guidance during implementation
- Technical risk assessment and escalation
- Code review participation and mentoring

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide executive alignment, decision authority, and resource approval. They represent business interests and ensure projects deliver expected value.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and business justification
- Make go/no-go decisions at key gates
- Provide or approve resources and budget
- Escalate blockers and organizational barriers
- Receive and review executive summaries of project status
- Validate that success metrics align with business goals

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value
- Maintain executive visibility and alignment
- Resolve organizational barriers to delivery

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and approval
- Monthly executive status updates
- Critical escalations and decision requests
- Post-project value realization review

---

## Release Manager / DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
Release Managers and DevOps Engineers orchestrate deployment activities, manage infrastructure, and ensure smooth production releases. They work with Developers, QA, and Operations to execute release plans and manage rollback scenarios.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and execute deployment activities
- Manage infrastructure, databases, and deployment pipelines
- Coordinate pre-release verifications and smoke tests
- Document and execute rollback procedures
- Monitor production health post-deployment
- Participate in incident response and troubleshooting

### Goals
- Execute releases with minimal risk and downtime
- Maintain production stability and performance
- Enable fast, automated deployment capabilities

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and checklist review
- Deployment day coordination
- Post-deployment verification and status
- Incident escalation and rollback decisions

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach (optional)

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or Agile Coaches facilitate team processes, remove impediments, and help the team continuously improve. They support the team in following agreed agile practices and help the organization adopt effective delivery patterns.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate standups, planning, retrospectives, and other agile ceremonies
- Help the team identify and remove impediments
- Coach the team on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Protect the team from unnecessary distractions and scope churn
- Track and surface process improvement opportunities

### Goals
- Improve team flow and predictability
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement
- Reduce blockers and improve meeting effectiveness

### Typical Communication
- Facilitation of team ceremonies
- Regular coaching sessions with the team
- Reporting on team health and process metrics
- Coordination with PMs and PdMs on process changes
